  • Koç görüşmeleriniz — koç motoru cihazınızda çalışır, sunucuya bağlanmaz
  • Sesiniz — konuşma tanıma cihaz içinde yapılır. Cihazınız cihaz içi tanımayı desteklemiyorsa bu özellik hiç açılmaz; ses sunucuya gönderilerek çözülmez.

3. Uyku hikâyeleri ve yapay zekâ

Uyku hikâyesi ürettiğinizde isteğiniz sunucumuz üzerinden Google Gemini'ye iletilir. Gönderilen tek şey seçtiğiniz tema, uzunluk ve dildir — üçü de önceden tanımlı seçeneklerdir. Yazdığınız hiçbir metin, ruh hâliniz veya kayıtlarınız gönderilmez. Bu aktarım öncesinde uygulama içinde ayrıca onayınız istenir; onay vermezseniz istek yapılmaz.

Privacy Policy

Effective: 07-09-2026 · Data controller: BUSATER YAZILIM A.Ş.

Summary. Your mood entries, CBT work, coach conversations and your voice never leave your device. Our server holds only your account, your purchase and content-free usage events. No ads, no tracking, no crash-reporting component.

1. Data sent to our server

DataWhyWhere
Email addressAccount creation and sign-inSupabase (backend)
NameReturned by Apple/Google at sign-inSupabase
Account ID (random UUID)Linking your account and subscriptionSupabase
Purchase recordVerifying entitlement, accountingSupabase · Apple · Google
Usage eventsMeasuring which screens are usedSupabase

Usage events come from a closed list and their properties can only be numbers or true/false. No text you write can enter these events; this is enforced by the application's type system.

2. Data that never leaves your device

  • Mood and daily entries
  • CBT / thought-record work
  • Coach conversations — the coach engine runs on your device and does not connect to a server
  • Your voice — speech recognition happens on-device. If your device does not support on-device recognition the feature is not offered at all; audio is never sent to a server instead.
  • Your preferences (goal, coach personality, language)

Our server schema contains no table for any of this.

3. Sleep stories and AI

When you generate a sleep story, the request goes through our server to Google Gemini. The only things sent are the theme, length and language you selected — all predefined options. No text you write, no mood data and no records are sent. The app asks for your consent before this transfer; without consent no request is made.

4. Third parties we use

PartyPurpose
SupabaseAccount, entitlement and usage events (backend)
Apple · GoogleSign in with Apple / Google
App Store · Google PlayPayments and subscription management
Google GeminiSleep-story text generation only, within the limits above

5. What we do not do

  • We show no ads and use no advertising identifier (IDFA).
  • We do not track you across apps or websites.
  • We do not sell your data.
  • We collect no crash reports, location, contacts, photos or health data.
  • We collect no push notification token.

6. Retention and deletion

You can delete your account in the app under Profile → Delete Account or from the account deletion page. Deletion removes your account, profile, entitlement records and event records. The purchase transaction record is not deleted; its link to your account is removed (the user-ID field is cleared). See the account deletion page for detail.

7. Your rights

Under GDPR and Turkish KVKK (Law 6698) you have rights of access, rectification, erasure and portability. Requests: bsdigitalapp+auberis@gmail.com

8. Children

Auberis is intended for users aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

9. Changes to this policy

We publish changes on this page and update the date. If the categories of data or the recipients change, your in-app consent is requested again.

Auberis is not a health service. It does not diagnose, does not provide treatment and does not replace a mental health professional. In an emergency call your local emergency number.
