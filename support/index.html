FAQ

How do I cancel my subscription?

On iOS: Settings → your Apple Account → Subscriptions. On Android: Play Store → Profile → Payments and subscriptions. Cancellation takes effect at the end of the current period.

My purchase is missing

Use Restore Purchases on the purchase screen and make sure you are signed in with the store account that made the purchase.

My entries do not appear on another device

For your privacy, mood, CBT and coach records are kept only on your device. They are not uploaded, so they do not sync across devices.

I want to delete my account

See the account deletion page.

Emergency. Auberis is not a health service and cannot respond to emergencies. If there is danger to life, call your local emergency number.
